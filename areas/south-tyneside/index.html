Exterior cleaning in South Tyneside

South Tyneside is a compact borough on the south bank of the Tyne. It runs from Hebburn and Jarrow, along the river, through South Shields to the coast, and inland to East Boldon, West Boldon, Boldon Colliery and Cleadon. That makes for a real mix of homes: older terraces and Tyneside flats, semi-detached family houses, post-war estates and some larger detached properties.

Because we work across the whole borough, nearby streets are easy to fit into the same day. Each town and village has its own page, with its postcodes, if you'd like the detail for your part of South Tyneside.
