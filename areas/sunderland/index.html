Exterior cleaning in Sunderland

Sunderland is a city on the Wear with its own stretch of coast. On the seafront at Roker and Seaburn, salt in the air leaves a film on windows and fascias; inland, suburbs such as Ashbrooke, Grindon and Silksworth have different needs, with older terraces and villas in some streets and large estates in others.

Sunderland's postcodes run from SR1 to SR6 and we cover all of them. Washington, which is part of the city but has NE postcodes, has its own page.
