Exterior cleaning in Jarrow

Jarrow stands on the south bank of the Tyne and is best known for the Venerable Bede, who lived and worked at the monastery here, and for the Jarrow March of 1936. The town is covered by a single postcode district, NE32.

Housing ranges from older streets of terraces and Tyneside flats near the river to later estates such as Fellgate. Terraces mean long runs of gutter and plenty of sills and frames; the estates tend to have wider soffits and fascias that go green over the years.
