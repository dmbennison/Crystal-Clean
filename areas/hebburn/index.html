Exterior cleaning in Hebburn

Hebburn lies on the south bank of the Tyne, just along the river from Jarrow, and has a long shipbuilding and engineering past. The whole town is in the NE31 postcode district.

Homes here are mostly terraces, Tyneside flats and post-war estates. Close to the river, shelter and damp air let green algae creep across fascias and soffits, particularly on the shadier sides of a house, so these are often the first thing people notice.
