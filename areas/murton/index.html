Exterior cleaning in Murton

Murton is a former colliery village in County Durham, a short drive inland from Seaham. It has the SR7 postcode shared with Seaham, and its own postcode sector, SR7 9.

Homes here include older terraced streets from the village's mining days alongside later housing. Older roofs and long terraced gutter runs collect moss and debris, and fascias and soffits darken with algae and dirt, all of which we can clear and clean.
